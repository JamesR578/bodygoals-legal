Community Guidelines

Body Goals · Last updated: 3 October 2026

1. Why these guidelines exist

Body Goals connects trainers and clients through profiles, messages, group chats and shared content. These guidelines apply anywhere you can post, message or interact with other users on the app. They exist to keep the platform safe, respectful, and trustworthy for everyone using it.

2. Be respectful

  • No harassment, bullying, threats, or targeted abuse of any user.
  • No hate speech or discrimination based on race, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability, or any other protected characteristic.
  • No sexual content, sexual harassment, or unwanted sexual attention.
  • Disagreements happen — keep them civil. Repeated hostility towards other users is not allowed.

3. Keep it safe

  • Don't share another person's private information (address, financial details, ID documents, etc.) without their consent.
  • Don't use the app to solicit payments, bookings, or business outside the platform in a way designed to avoid fees or bypass safety checks.
  • No spam, scams, phishing links, or misleading offers.
  • No impersonating another trainer, client, or Body Goals staff member.

4. Trainer conduct

  • Only list qualifications, certifications, or experience you actually hold. False claims may result in removal from the platform.
  • Don't give medical advice, diagnose conditions, or promote unsafe dieting, supplement use, or training practices.
  • Treat every client professionally, regardless of their fitness level or goals.

5. Content you post

  • No illegal content, or content that promotes illegal activity.
  • No graphic violence, self-harm content, or material that could encourage harm to others.
  • Only post content you have the rights to share. Don't upload copyrighted material you don't own.
  • Group chats and shared files should stay relevant to fitness, training, and the purpose of the group.

6. Reporting and blocking

If another user breaks these guidelines, you can report them or specific content directly in the app, and you can block a user at any time to stop them contacting you. Reports are reviewed, and we may remove content, restrict features, suspend, or permanently remove an account depending on the severity and whether it's a repeat issue.

7. Enforcement

We review reports as they come in. Action taken depends on the nature and severity of the issue, and can include a warning, content removal, a temporary restriction, or permanent removal from Body Goals. Accounts that pose a safety risk to others may be removed immediately without prior warning.

8. Appeals

If you believe a decision about your account or content was made in error, you can contact us at rigbyjames100+support@gmail.com to request a review.

9. Changes to these guidelines

We may update these guidelines as the app evolves. Material changes will be flagged in the app.
