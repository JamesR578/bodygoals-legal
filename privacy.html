Privacy Policy

Body Goals · Last updated: 10 August 2026

1. Who we are

Body Goals is operated by James Maxwell Rigby, trading as Body Goals, a sole trader based in the United Kingdom. Our business address is 1A Sumner Avenue, Bolton, BL2 5RF.

For any privacy questions, contact us at rigbyjames100@gmail.com.

2. What data we collect

Account information: your name, email address, password (handled securely by Supabase), and profile/verification photos.

Profile information: your role (trainer or client), specialties, fitness goals, gym affiliation, location text, and bio.

Photos: profile pictures and, for clients, progress photos you choose to upload.

Verification documents (trainers only): government-issued ID photos (passport, driving licence), selfies for identity matching, professional certification documents, insurance policy documents, and tax identification numbers (UTR). These are collected for verification, trust & safety, and tax compliance purposes.

Activity data: sessions booked, goals, body measurements, messages sent within the app, and reviews.

Device & usage data: basic technical information needed to run the app (e.g. crash logs), where you have analytics tracking enabled.

3. How we use your data

To create and manage your account, and to provide the core service (connecting trainers and clients).

To process bookings, display your sessions, and enable communication between trainers and clients.

To send you notifications you have opted into (see Notification Settings).

To respond to support requests sent via Help & Support.

To improve the app, only where you have enabled analytics tracking in Privacy Settings.

4. Who we share data with

Supabase: our database and authentication provider. They process data on our behalf and do not use it for their own purposes.

Stripe: in-app payments are processed via Stripe. Payment details are handled directly by Stripe; the app only stores the card brand, last 4 digits, and expiry for saved payment methods.

Other users: your profile (and, if you are a trainer, your reviews) is visible to other users as part of the core function of the app, subject to your Profile Visibility setting.

Verification documents (trainers only): your ID photos, certification documents, insurance policies, and tax information are viewed only by the app admin for verification purposes and are not shared with other users or third parties.

We do not sell your personal data to third parties, and we do not share it for third-party marketing without your consent (see Third-party Sharing in Privacy Settings).

5. Data retention

We keep your data for as long as your account is active.

If you request account deletion, we process this within 30 days, after which your personal data is permanently removed, save for any records we are legally required to retain (e.g. for tax or fraud-prevention purposes).

6. Your rights

You can access and export a copy of your data at any time from Privacy & Data > Export Your Data.

You can update your profile information at any time from Profile Settings.

You can request deletion of your account and data from Privacy & Data > Delete Account.

You can withdraw consent for marketing emails, analytics, location tracking, and data sharing at any time from Privacy Settings.

If you are in the UK or EU, you have rights under UK GDPR / GDPR including the right to object, restrict processing, and lodge a complaint with the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

If you are in the US, we honour equivalent rights to access, delete, and opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information (we do not sell personal data).

7. Age requirement

You must be at least 18 years old to use Body Goals. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under this age.

8. Security

Data is transmitted using encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) and stored using our infrastructure provider's security controls.

No system is 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be flagged in the app.
