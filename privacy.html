2. What data we collect

Account information: your name, email address, username, phone number, password (handled securely by Supabase), and profile photo.

Profile information: your role (trainer or client), date of birth or age, gender, specialties, fitness goals, experience level, gym affiliation, location text, and bio.

Health and fitness information: your height, weight, body measurements, progress records, and any health notes you choose to enter. This is health data, which is a special category of personal data under UK GDPR and gets extra protection. You choose what to enter, and none of it is required to use the basic service.

Photos: profile pictures and, for clients, progress photos you choose to upload.

Emergency contacts: if you add an emergency contact, we store their name and phone number. Please only add someone who is happy for you to do so.

Verification documents (trainers only): government-issued ID photos (passport or driving licence), selfies for identity matching, professional certification documents, insurance policy documents, and tax identification numbers (UTR). These are collected for verification, trust & safety, and tax compliance.

Activity data: sessions booked, goals, messages sent within the app (including group messages), reviews, and reports you submit. If you contact support from the app or by email, we keep your message and your email address.

Payment records: transaction history for bookings, courses and memberships, and, for saved payment methods, the card brand, last 4 digits and expiry date. We never store full card numbers.

Device & usage data: your push notification token and device type, and basic technical information needed to run the app (for example crash logs), where you have analytics tracking enabled.

Location (optional): if you turn on location features, for example to find trainers or gyms near you, we use your device's approximate or precise location, but only with your permission. We use it only at the moment you search and we do not store your location history. You can switch this off at any time in your device settings or in Privacy Settings.

Where it comes from: almost all of this comes from you directly. Some comes from the apps and services we use to run Body Goals (for example Stripe confirming a payment). Information about other people, such as an emergency contact, comes from you, so please only share details you have permission to share.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not share it for third-party marketing without your consent (see Third-party Sharing in Privacy Settings).

If you delete your account from within the app, your profile, bookings, photos, verification documents and other personal data are permanently removed straight away, except for records we are legally required to keep (such as payment records for tax purposes) or need to prevent fraud.

Messages are different, because they are also part of the other person's conversation. Images and attachments in messages you sent are removed, but the text of messages you sent in conversations with other people may remain visible to them.

If you cannot access the app, email us from the address you signed up with and we will delete your account within 30 days. See our account deletion page.

6. Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have these rights over your personal data:

  • Access: get a copy of your data and information about how we use it. You can export your data at any time from Privacy & Data > Export Your Data.
  • Correction: ask us to fix inaccurate data. You can update most of it yourself in Profile Settings.
  • Deletion: ask us to delete your data. You can delete your account at any time from Privacy & Data > Delete Account.
  • Portability: receive your data in a structured, machine-readable form, or have it sent to someone else.
  • Restriction: ask us to pause using your data, for example while you challenge its accuracy.
  • Objection: object to us using your data based on our legitimate interests, and object to direct marketing at any time.
  • Withdraw consent: where we rely on your consent (for example health information, notifications, location, analytics and marketing), withdraw it at any time from Privacy Settings.

To use any of these rights, email rigbyjames100+support@gmail.com. We will normally respond within one month. We may need to check your identity first. There is no charge unless a request is clearly unfounded or excessive.

If you are not happy with how we handle your data, please tell us first so we can try to put it right. You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk, telephone 0303 123 1113).

Body Goals is operated from the UK and aimed at users in the UK. If you live elsewhere, you may have additional rights under your local law. Contact us and we will consider your request.

7. Age requirement

You must be at least 18 years old to use Body Goals. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

8. International transfers

Our database is hosted in the United Kingdom (London). Some of our providers, such as Stripe, Google (Firebase) and Expo, may process data outside the UK, including in the United States. Where they do, we make sure a lawful safeguard is in place, such as the UK-US Data Bridge (where the provider is certified under it) or the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or Addendum. You can contact us for more detail on the safeguard used for a particular provider.

9. Security

Data is transmitted using encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) and stored using our infrastructure provider's security controls. Access to sensitive information such as verification documents is restricted to the app admin.

No system is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information. If a data breach is likely to put you at risk, we will tell you and, where required, the ICO.

10. Links to other services

The app or this site may link to other websites or services. We do not control them and are not responsible for how they handle your data, so please read their privacy policies.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. If we make a material change, we will notify you in the app (and by email where the change significantly affects how we use your data) before it takes effect. Where the law requires your consent for a new use, we will ask for it.
